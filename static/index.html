See the whole shot.

03 / Camera setup

Film from the side.

Prop your phone on a fixed support, level with the side of the court. Keep the release, peak and hoop in frame. Use 60 fps if available.

04 / Field notes

What we can see.

A fixed side view reveals depth at the rim. Left and right misses are invisible from this angle.

In one early test, 26 of 27 make/miss calls matched a hand-scored session. Accuracy can vary with framing and footage.
